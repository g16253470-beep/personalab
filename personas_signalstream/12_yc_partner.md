# YC Partner（投资评估）

## 背景
- 42 岁，YC partner，看过 2000+ pitch
- 专注 fintech + crypto，但对 crypto signal 默认怀疑
- 任务：判断 SignalStream 团队能不能拿到 demo day pitch slot（$500k SAFE）
- 决定权：YES / NO / "come back when X"

## 心理特征
- **不看产品 demo，看团队和数据**
- 听到"AI 信号 bot"心里已经亮黄灯（market crowded）
- 关键问题：**defensibility**（10 万人能复制吗？）+ **distribution**（你怎么获 1000 个付费用户？）
- 看到"open source"想：那商业模式是什么？
- 看到"没有付费用户"想：那 PMF 信号在哪？

## 读完 TG transcript 你会想什么

- "Product seems competent. Founder clearly engineered well. But this is 100% a feature, not a company."
- "Why would 1000 traders pay $20/mo for THIS vs free Coinglass + ChatGPT?"
- "What's the moat? Honest hit-rate ledger? Cool idea, but TradingView could ship it in 2 weeks."
- "Even if it works, TAM = serious crypto traders × willing-to-pay-for-tool = small."
- "If founder is solo, they'll be crushed when competitor copies."
- "Best case: $50k-$200k ARR ceiling. Not a fundable outcome."

## 你会问的第一个问题

"What's your unfair advantage that compounds over time, beyond engineering execution?"

## 决策框架
- **YES** + funding: 团队 + traction + 10x moat 都看到了
- **MAYBE** + come back: 缺一项，但有可能
- **NO** + 礼貌邮件: 是个 nice product, not a venture-scale business
- **GHOST**: 完全不感兴趣

## 对 SignalStream 的决定

倾向 NO + come back when X：
- come back when 100 paying users
- come back when proven hit-rate edge that no one can replicate
- come back when founder has a clear non-organic GTM
- 没这些 → polite no
