## 🗣 详细反馈

### 01_early_founder

- **会订阅**: yes
- **退订风险**: 8/10
- **愿付月费**: 5-20
- **吐槽**: Cal.com的免费版 Branding 导致客户认为这是垃圾邮件或第三方工具，这对我Pre-seed阶段的fake door测试是致命打击。我不能让任何环节显得不专业。
- **欣赏**: 个人使用流程很顺畅，10分钟搞定，12刀去品牌和加Workflow很划算，开源也加分。
- **TOP 3 改进**:
  - 给新用户提供14天无品牌试用，或者更便宜的去品牌方案，比如 $5/月。
  - 团队协作功能需要更明确的价值主张，尤其要解决对现有 M365/Google Workspace 集成方案的比较劣势。
  - 简化 self-host 的运维门槛，比如提供一个托管方案，或者更简单的部署脚本。
- **原话**:
  > 妈的，这Cal.com个人用起来挺顺手，10分钟搞定，连Zoom都自动配置了。但发给客户人家一看 Branding 就犹豫，这不是给我找麻烦吗？我刚被客户放鸽子，最怕这种不专业感。算了，我自己掏12刀去 Branding 算了，跑 fake door test 容不得这种细节翻车。但想推给团队就尴尬了，老板直接一句'我们有 Bookings'就把我顶死了，Sales 觉得那些高级功能不值60刀。行吧，就我自己用，团队还是 Bookings，搞得我像个个人布道者。这和上次我做的那个死于假需求的产品有啥区别？关键是，这个$12到底值不值，别到时候又没人用。

- **TOP 3 改进**:
  - 提供更灵活的免费品牌露出策略，降低首次外部互动时的感知摩擦。
  - 强化团队版针对 M365 Bookings 等免费竞品的差异化价值主张，量化效率和转化提升。
  - 为销售团队提供更多特定功能（如A/B测试不同日程链接），直接赋能转化OKR。
- **原话**:
  > I evaluated Cal.com on 3 axes: individual utility, team scalability, and conversion impact. Individual utility is 8/10; fast, clean, feature-rich. Team scalability is 3/10; failed to overcome M365 Bookings, weak value prop. Conversion impact is 5/10 due to initial branding friction. Overall, strong for solo use, but adoption at the team level is a hard sell against 'free' alternatives. Needs a clearer, data-backed ROI narrative for B2B.

### 03_user_researcher_hostile

- **会订阅**: no
- **退订风险**: 3/10
- **愿付月费**: 0
- **吐槽**: The immediate client query about 'third-party tool or spam' due to the unavoidable branding on the free tier is a critical user trust issue, directly impacting perceived professionalism and adoption, especially for consultants interacting with new clients.
- **欣赏**: The personal scheduling workflow is clean and efficient. Conceptually, the open-source nature and self-hosting option are commendable for control and data ownership, aligning with best practices for digital tools.
- **TOP 3 改进**:
  - Offer a genuinely professional free tier or a very low-cost professional branding upgrade, acknowledging the critical role of trust and legitimacy in client interactions and avoiding the 'spam' perception.
  - Develop a rigorous, empirically validated value proposition for teams that explicitly addresses and quantifies benefits over *already-owned* bundled solutions like M365 Bookings, focusing on specific pain points and ROI.
  - Invest in brand recognition or provide users with clear, evidence-based messaging to pre-empt client skepticism about using a less-known scheduling tool, addressing 'recognition rather than recall' heuristics.
- **原话**:
  > As someone who's conducted 200+ user interviews, the Cal.com experience perfectly illustrates Jakob Nielsen's usability heuristics – particularly 'Recognition rather than recall' and 'Match between system and the real world.' A client asking if it's 'spam' because of branding isn't a minor glitch; it’s a fundamental breach of trust and professionalism. Furthermore, the inability to clearly articulate incremental value over *existing, zero-marginal-cost* solutions like Microsoft Bookings for a team highlights a failure to understand organizational procurement and the real 'jobs to be done.' You’re selling features, not addressing the critical 'unknown unknowns' that real user research uncovers. This isn't just about price; it's about perceived value and, frankly, professional malpractice if adopted without proper due diligence.

  - 考虑将核心调度引擎或组件剥离为 MIT/Apache 2.0 许可，以鼓励更广泛的集成和采用，而非全栈 AGPLv3。

  - 提供更深入的 `ARCHITECTURE.md`，解释项目设计决策，而非仅描述结构，方便社区贡献者理解和上手。

  > Cal.com 的定位和开源属性很吸引我。AGPLv3 许可不如 MIT 灵活，但完整的 self-host 选项，Docker Compose 就能跑，且强调 'own your data'，这是核心优势。它完美规避了云端付费去 branding 的痛点，提供了真正的独立部署可能。我个人绝不会为 'open core' 模型的免费云服务 branding 付费。下一步我会 clone 仓库，深入审阅 `/core/` 代码抽象、依赖健康度，并查看 `/tests/` 覆盖率。若质量符合预期，我会提个非破坏性 PR 观察维护者响应。之后，我会将 Cal.com 推荐到我的 dev tool newsletter，特别是给那些愿意为解决方案付费的 founder 和 PM。
